CLEARDESK V10 — FORM EMAIL DELIVERY

The Contact and Careers forms now submit directly to info@cleardesk.co.nz.
The Careers form also accepts an optional PDF or Word CV up to 10 MB.
Both forms redirect successful visitors to /thank-you/.

ONE-TIME ACTIVATION AFTER GITHUB UPLOAD
1. Open the live Contact page.
2. Complete and submit one test enquiry using your own details.
3. Open the inbox for info@cleardesk.co.nz (also check Spam/Junk).
4. Open the activation email from FormSubmit.
5. Click Activate Form.
6. Return to the website and submit a second test enquiry.
7. Confirm the second test arrives with all form details.
8. Test the Careers form in the same way. If another activation email arrives,
   click its activation link too.

Keep the activation email. Mark future FormSubmit messages as Not Spam if needed.
The website uses the free FormSubmit service because GitHub Pages cannot send
emails by itself. CAPTCHA and a hidden spam trap are enabled.

UPLOAD
Extract the ZIP. Upload its CONTENTS to the root of the GitHub repository.
Allow replacement of existing files. Do not delete the existing CNAME file.
Confirm index.html is visible on the repository front page.
