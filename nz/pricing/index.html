New Zealand pricing

Flat monthly fees, quoted before we start

No hourly billing and no surprise invoices. You know the number before anything begins.

Prices are being confirmed

Our New Zealand package prices are under review and will be published here once the Director has confirmed them. We have deliberately not left old figures on the page — an out-of-date price is worse than no price. Message us and you will have a written quote within one working day.

How our pricing works

  • Flat monthly fee per desk, based on your transaction volume and what is included — not an hourly rate.
  • Quoted in writing before we start, within one working day of you telling us what you need.
  • Catch-up work is priced separately and fixed before it begins, once we know how many periods are involved.
  • Software is not included. Xero and MYOB subscriptions are separate. You hold your own, or we arrange one at cost with no margin added.

What we need to quote you

  1. What your business does, and which desk fits — Driver, Trade, Business or Host
  2. Roughly how many transactions a month
  3. Whether you are up to date, and if not, how far back it goes
  4. What software you use, if any
  5. Whether you have employees

Send those five things on BOTIM and you will have a number back inside a working day.
