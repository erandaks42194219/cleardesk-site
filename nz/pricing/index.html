How quotation works

A fair scope starts with five facts

1

Business and desk

What you do and which package is the closest fit.

2

Monthly workload

Transactions, bank accounts, invoices and employees.

3

Record position

Whether the books are current and any catch-up periods.

4

Systems

Xero, MYOB, spreadsheets, connected apps and automation needs.

5

Service level

Reporting, payroll, AP/AR and the rhythm of updates you need.

What your proposal shows

  • Included tasks and frequency
  • Client and ClearDesk responsibilities
  • Catch-up or project work separated from recurring work
  • Software and third-party costs identified separately
  • A private fee based on the agreed scope
