Confidential capacity for accounting practices

More bookkeeping capacity. Your client relationship stays protected.

ClearDesk works behind your accounting firm to process agreed bookkeeping and finance-administration work. Your practice remains the client’s trusted adviser, primary contact and decision-maker.

The ClearDesk boundary

We support your firm—not compete with it.

  • We communicate through your nominated practice contact.
  • We do not solicit or market to your clients.
  • We do not discuss fees or offer separate services to them.
  • Any direct client contact requires your specific written instruction.
  • Work is returned to your team for review and approval.

How control is maintained

Your standards, documented into the workflow

Before work begins, both parties agree the scope, systems, access, deadlines, review points and escalation path.

  1. 1

    Define

    Choose the files and tasks suitable for delegation.

  2. 2

    Document

    Record your coding rules, workpaper expectations and approval boundaries.

  3. 3

    Pilot

    Complete a small sample and refine the handover.

  4. 4

    Deliver

    Return completed work, open queries and an exception summary to your reviewer.

Commercial and client safeguards

  • Non-solicitation: ClearDesk does not approach your clients for separate work.
  • Firm-led communication: questions and deliverables flow through your nominated contact.
  • Controlled access: access is limited to the systems and information needed for the agreed work.
  • Written responsibilities: scope, review ownership, deadlines and exceptions are confirmed before commencement.
  • Review remains with your firm: your practice approves the final work and controls all client advice.
