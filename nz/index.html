Worth knowing

If your income comes through a platform, Inland Revenue already sees it

Since 1 January 2024, New Zealand-resident marketplaces — Uber, Airbnb, Bookabach, DoorDash and others — report seller income to Inland Revenue each year by 7 February.

We mention this because a lot of people genuinely do not know, and finding out late is worse than finding out now. It is not a reason to panic. It is a reason to make sure what you file matches what they already have.

If you are behind, that is a common position and a fixable one. Tell us how far back it goes and we will quote the catch-up as a fixed fee before starting.

Dates worth having in your calendar

7 FebruaryMarketplaces report seller income to Inland Revenue
28 FebruaryGST return and payment for many filers
31 MarchEnd of the standard tax year
7 AprilTerminal tax for the previous year
7 JulyIR3 income tax return due if you are not with an agent
28 AugustProvisional tax instalment for many filers

Your own dates depend on your filing frequency and balance date. Confirm yours in myIR or ask us.
