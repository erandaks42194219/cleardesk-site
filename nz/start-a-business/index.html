01 / 6 services

Set up the business

A considered start, with the right paperwork.

Discuss these services ↗
01.01

Startup planning session

A 30-minute scoping session and personalised action checklist. General information, not legal or financial product advice.

01.02

Business name checks

Check your preferred name against the Companies Register and available domains. Not a trademark clearance or a guarantee of availability.

01.03

Name reservation support

Assist with one company name reservation. Official reservation fee is separate. Subject to acceptance and compliance review.

01.04

Company registration support

Support one standard NZ company with up to two individual directors/shareholders. Subject to eligibility, authority and AML/CFT checks. Legal structuring and registered office services excluded.

01.05

Bank application preparation

Prepare the document checklist for one business bank account application. You choose the bank and complete identity checks. No product recommendation or approval guarantee.

01.06

IRD, NZBN and GST setup review

Check IRD/NZBN setup steps and GST registration requirements. Assist with an application where appropriate and authorised. Personalised tax advice and returns excluded.

Create your identity

02.01

Starter wordmark logo

One text-based logo direction and two revision rounds. SVG and PNG files included. Custom illustration and trademark registration excluded.

02.02

Mini brand guide

One-page colour and font guide using your agreed identity. Requires a supplied or selected logo.

02.03

Business card design

One double-sided business card design and two revision rounds. Print-ready artwork supplied.

02.04

500 printed business cards

Standard double-sided cards, one design, delivered to one NZ address. Print-ready artwork required; special finishes and extra delivery charges quoted first.

02.05

Email signature

One branded signature template and setup guidance for one person.

03 / 6 services

Build your online home

A practical website that works on a phone.

Discuss these services ↗
03.01

Three-page starter website

Home, Services and Contact pages in a mobile-friendly static layout. One design direction, two revision rounds and launch on an agreed host. No shop, booking system or custom integrations.

03.02

Website copy tidy-up

Edit up to 600 words from your supplied business information. Research, translations and legal policies excluded.

03.03

Basic search setup

Page titles, descriptions and Google Search Console connection for one compatible website. No ranking guarantee.

03.04

Enquiry form setup

One contact form with up to five fields on a compatible website. Client-owned form service; any subscription is separate. No sensitive information fields.

03.05

First-year domain allowance

Up to NZ$40 excluding GST towards one available domain for the first year. Any excess agreed first. Renewal charges separate; domain registered for the client.

03.06

Business email setup

Configure one mailbox and domain email records with a client-selected provider. Email subscription and migration of existing mail excluded.

Get found and recognised

Useful profiles, ready for your first customers.

04.01

Google Business Profile setup

Profile setup and verification guidance for eligible in-person or service-area businesses. Online-only businesses are not eligible. Google controls verification; no approval or ranking guarantee.

04.02

Facebook business page

Set up or tidy one page using your supplied details, logo and contact links. You own and approve the page.

04.03

Instagram business setup

Set up one business profile and link to your Facebook page where eligible. Access and platform approval required.

04.04

Social launch design kit

One cover design and three editable launch-post templates using your agreed brand. Ongoing posting, advertising spend and photography excluded.

Organise the money and handover

Clear records from the first invoice.

05.01

Accounting file setup

Create one new Xero or MYOB file with a starter chart of accounts. Subscription paid separately by you. Migration, historical clean-up and tax returns excluded.

05.02

One bank feed connection

Help connect one supported account to your accounting file. Bank availability and your authorisation required. We do not handle banking passwords.

05.03

Invoice and quote templates

Configure one branded invoice and one quote template in a compatible accounting system.

Receipt and document folders

Set up a simple client-owned cloud folder structure and receipt naming routine. Cloud storage subscription, if needed, is separate.

Startup cash-flow planner

A simple 12-month cash-flow template using your assumptions, plus guidance. Not a forecast guarantee or personalised financial advice.

05.06

Business obligations calendar

An initial calendar of agreed filing and renewal reminders based on your business details. You remain responsible for deadlines; ongoing monitoring excluded.

05.07

Owner handover session

One 60-minute online walkthrough of the systems selected in this quote, with handover notes.

30-day setup support

Up to one hour of remote help with the delivered setup during the first 30 days after handover. Additional work quoted separately.
