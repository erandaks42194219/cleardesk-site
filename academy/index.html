Explore the skills

Practical skills behind everyday accounting work.

01

Practical bookkeeping

Transaction records, source documents, bank reconciliations, accounts payable and receivable, and a practical month-end checklist.

Practise: prepare a cashbook and reconciliation using fictional transactions.
02

Xero & MYOB workflows

System navigation, invoices, bills, bank coding, reconciliations and reporting. Software access and any licence requirements are confirmed before enrolment.

Practise: follow an example transaction through to a useful report.
03

NZ GST & IRD workflows

GST records, transaction classification, supporting workpapers, an introduction to myIR and the distinction between filing and payment responsibilities.

Practise: build a NZ GST workpaper from sample data. Educational content; not personal tax advice.
04

Independent IPA / IFA guidance

Understand published membership pathways, organise your document checklist and identify questions to confirm directly with the relevant professional body.

Take away: a checklist of requirements to investigate. Membership decisions remain with IPA / IFA.

Before you enquire

Can I get Sinhala support?

Yes. Training is delivered in English with Sinhala support. Tell us the language support you need when you enquire.

What does training cost, and when does it start?

We confirm scope, duration, delivery format and availability in writing. You receive the currency, applicable taxes, software costs and cancellation terms before payment. An enquiry is not a paid booking.

Does this include Australian tax or BAS training?

The advertised GST and IRD content is specific to New Zealand. It does not imply Australian tax or BAS training. Tell us your destination and learning goals so we can confirm what the training covers.

Will this guarantee a job or professional membership?

No. This is independent practical education. We do not guarantee jobs, visas, migration outcomes, membership or vendor certification. No endorsement or partnership with IPA, IFA or software vendors is claimed.
