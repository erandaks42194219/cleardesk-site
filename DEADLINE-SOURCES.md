# Deadline carousel source notes

Checked 5 September 2026. These are internal maintenance notes, not a substitute
for checking the relevant authority and the client's own tax period before advice
is given.

## United Arab Emirates

- **E-invoicing:** UAE Ministry of Finance, implementation timeline. Businesses with
  annual revenue below AED 50 million must appoint an Accredited Service Provider by
  31 March 2027 and implement from 1 July 2027.
  https://mof.gov.ae/en/news/ministry-of-finance-announces-the-issuance-of-two-ministerial-decisions-on-the-scope-of-obligations-and-the-timelines-for-implementing-the-electronic-invoicing-system-2/
- **Corporate Tax:** UAE Federal Tax Authority. Corporate Tax returns and payment are
  generally due within nine months after the end of the relevant tax period.
  https://tax.gov.ae/en/media.centre/news/federal.tax.authority.urges.submission.of.corporate.tax.returns.and.settlement.of.corporate.tax.liabilities.within.nine.months.from.the.end.of.the.tax.period.aspx
- **VAT:** UAE Federal Tax Authority. VAT returns and related payments are due within
  28 days after the end of the tax period.
  https://tax.gov.ae/en/taxes/Vat/vat.topics/filing.vat.returns.and.making.payments.aspx
- **Small Business Relief:** UAE Ministry of Finance. The AED 3 million revenue
  threshold applies to eligible tax periods ending on or before 31 December 2026,
  subject to the published conditions.
  https://mof.gov.ae/en/news/ministry-of-finance-issues-decision-on-small-business-relief-for-corporate-tax-purposes/

## New Zealand

- **GST:** Inland Revenue. Returns and payment are generally due on the 28th after
  the taxable period, with 15 January and 7 May exceptions for the specified periods.
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/gst/filing-and-paying-gst-and-refunds
- **IR3:** Inland Revenue. Returns are generally due 7 July unless the taxpayer has
  a tax agent or another extension of time.
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-individuals/what-happens-at-the-end-of-the-tax-year/individual-income-tax-return---ir3
- **Payday filing:** Inland Revenue. Electronic employment information is due within
  two working days of each payday.
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/employing-staff/payday-filing

Review these sources before changing a deadline and at least quarterly while the
deadline carousel remains published.
