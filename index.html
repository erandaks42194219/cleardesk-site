🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates

E-invoicing is mandatory. The dates are set.

Businesses under AED 50 million must appoint an accredited service provider by 31 March 2027 and be issuing structured PINT AE invoices by 1 July 2027. Separately, Small Business Relief ends on 31 December 2026.

 🇳🇿 New Zealand

E-invoicing is optional. It is also faster money.

There is no mandate for private businesses here. But around 135 government agencies now pay e-invoices within 5 business days instead of 10 — and we still do the bookkeeping, GST and back office that keeps the rest of it quiet.
