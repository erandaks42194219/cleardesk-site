## Claims deliberately excluded

- Competitor prices or salary ranges that may become stale.
- A promise that outsourcing is always cheaper than an employee.
- A promise of full compliance in every jurisdiction or free zone.
- A promise that all NZ government eInvoices will be paid in five business days.
- Claims that ClearDesk is an accredited e-invoicing service provider, tax agent, government-approved provider or law firm.
- Invented vacancies, testimonials, certifications, response times or local-office claims.
