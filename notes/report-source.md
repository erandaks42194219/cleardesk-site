# ClearDesk Version 7 — market and policy research source

**Prepared:** 5 September 2026  
**Audience:** ClearDesk leadership and future website editors  
**Purpose:** Support the Version 7 website changes with current, primary-source research for New Zealand and the United Arab Emirates.

## Executive answer

ClearDesk should not compete through a public price list. Workload, entity count, transaction volume, payroll, catch-up periods, systems and reporting needs materially change delivery effort. The site therefore presents recognisable service packages while moving every commercial fee to a written, client-specific quotation.

The stronger market position is a managed finance-operations service: documented monthly work, scalable capacity, continuity beyond one employee, and coordinated bookkeeping, tax preparation, e-invoicing readiness and automation. This is a fairer comparison with an in-house hire than unverified salary or competitor-price claims.

The website must also show credible information-handling controls without promising universal legal compliance. ClearDesk serves clients across jurisdictions, so the public policy framework uses phrases such as “where applicable” and makes clear that the signed engagement, client instructions and relevant law determine the final obligations.

## UAE findings and website implications

### E-invoicing creates an implementation workload, not only a software purchase

The UAE Ministry of Finance has published phased appointment and implementation dates. For businesses with revenue below AED 50 million, the published dates are 31 March 2027 to appoint an accredited service provider and 1 July 2027 to implement. The website should therefore present readiness, master-data cleanup, field mapping, integration coordination, testing and monitoring as distinct workstreams—not imply that ClearDesk itself performs the accredited transmission role. [UAE Ministry of Finance — implementation scope and timelines](https://mof.gov.ae/en/news/ministry-of-finance-announces-the-issuance-of-two-ministerial-decisions-on-the-scope-of-obligations-and-the-timelines-for-implementing-the-electronic-invoicing-system-2/)

### Tax and VAT deadlines reinforce the value of maintained records

Corporate Tax returns and payments are generally due within nine months from the end of the relevant tax period. VAT returns and payments are generally due within 28 days from the end of the tax period. These requirements support a recurring calendar, monthly close and exception-follow-up message rather than a once-a-year filing message. [Federal Tax Authority — Corporate Tax filing timing](https://tax.gov.ae/en/media.centre/news/federal.tax.authority.urges.submission.of.corporate.tax.returns.and.settlement.of.corporate.tax.liabilities.within.nine.months.from.the.end.of.the.tax.period.aspx) and [Federal Tax Authority — filing VAT returns and making payments](https://tax.gov.ae/en/taxes/Vat/vat.topics/filing.vat.returns.and.making.payments.aspx)

Small Business Relief covers eligible tax periods ending on or before 31 December 2026, subject to its conditions. The site should treat 2027 as a preparation point and avoid implying automatic eligibility. [UAE Ministry of Finance — Small Business Relief](https://mof.gov.ae/en/news/ministry-of-finance-issues-decision-on-small-business-relief-for-corporate-tax-purposes/)

### Records and privacy need explicit controls

The Federal Tax Authority states that Corporate Tax records and documents generally need to be retained for at least seven years after the end of the relevant tax period. The policy page therefore uses a jurisdiction-specific retention rule rather than a single worldwide deletion period. [Federal Tax Authority — Corporate Tax record retention](https://tax.gov.ae/en/media.centre/news/pr.28082025.aspx)

The UAE Personal Data Protection Law regulates personal-data processing and cross-border transfers, while sector and free-zone regimes may also apply. Public wording should cover purpose limitation, controlled access, security, rights requests and cross-border handling, but it should not make a blanket “fully compliant” claim for every client or data set. [UAE Government — data protection laws](https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/data/data-protection-laws) and [UAE Legislation — Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1972/download)

### Outsourcing comparison must be accurate and restrained

MOHRE requires the appropriate work-permit process for employment. An in-house finance hire also creates employer-managed recruitment, payroll, leave, equipment, supervision and continuity responsibilities. The comparison page should describe these operational obligations without asserting that outsourcing is always cheaper or suitable for every business. [MOHRE — recruiting a worker from overseas](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/en/services/recruiting-a-worker-from-overseas-2022)

## New Zealand findings and website implications

### Small-business digital capacity remains a real constraint

MBIE has reported that New Zealand SMEs lag internationally in digitalisation and identifies barriers including cybersecurity concerns and lack of time. ClearDesk’s message should focus on practical implementation, owner time and connected finance workflows—not technology for its own sake. [MBIE — Briefing for the Incoming Minister for Small Business and Manufacturing](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/dmsdocument/30509-briefing-for-the-incoming-minister-for-small-business-and-manufacturing-proactiverelease-pdf) and [MBIE — Understanding the digital capability of New Zealand businesses](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/dmsdocument/17055-understanding-the-digital-capability-of-new-zealand-businesses)

### E-invoicing has a cash-flow use case for government suppliers

From 1 January 2026, mandated agencies are expected to pay 95% of eligible domestic trade eInvoices within five business days. This supports a specific benefit for eligible government suppliers, but the site must not promise that every invoice or every customer will be paid within five days. [New Zealand eInvoicing — government rules take effect](https://www.einvoicing.govt.nz/news-and-updates/government-einvoicing-and-prompt-payment-rules-take-effect) and [New Zealand eInvoicing — improving payment times](https://www.einvoicing.govt.nz/get-set-up/advice-for-government-agencies/improving-payment-times)

### Record retention and offshore systems require care

Inland Revenue says business records generally need to be kept for at least seven tax years, and businesses using offshore or cloud storage need to consider the applicable approval and access requirements. Records must remain retrievable and readable, with adequate backup arrangements. The policy page therefore commits to a documented retention schedule and accessible records rather than naming one storage vendor. [Inland Revenue — record keeping](https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/record-keeping) and [Inland Revenue — computer record keeping for audit](https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/record-keeping/computer-record-keeping-for-audit)

### Cross-border privacy and breach response are central to remote delivery

Information Privacy Principle 12 restricts disclosure of personal information outside New Zealand unless the recipient or arrangement provides the required safeguards, or another permitted basis applies. Notifiable privacy breaches that have caused or are likely to cause serious harm must be notified to the Privacy Commissioner and affected people as soon as practicable. ClearDesk should therefore establish client-approved data locations, supplier checks, an incident-assessment pathway and clear escalation contacts. [Office of the Privacy Commissioner — IPP 12](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/12/), [sending information overseas](https://www.privacy.org.nz/responsibilities/disclosing-personal-information-outside-new-zealand/) and [privacy breaches](https://www.privacy.org.nz/responsibilities/privacy-breaches/)

### In-house hiring carries continuing employer administration

New Zealand employers must manage written agreements, minimum rights, pay and leave, and accurate wage/time/leave records. Government guidance also asks employers to consider the full cost and responsibility before hiring. The comparison page should show that ClearDesk can add finance capacity without creating another employment position, while acknowledging that an internal hire may be preferable where daily on-site control or a full-time strategic role is required. [Employment New Zealand — hiring your first employee](https://www.employment.govt.nz/starting-employment/hiring/hiring-your-first-employee) and [Business.govt.nz — hiring for the first time](https://www.business.govt.nz/people-and-leave/deciding-to-hire/hiring-for-the-first-time)

## Policy design adopted for Version 7

- Data minimisation and purpose-limited use.
- Role-based access and confidentiality obligations.
- MFA where available, approved systems, device safeguards, secure transfer and backup controls.
- Jurisdiction- and engagement-specific retention; secure return or deletion when retention is no longer required.
- Cross-border handling only under client instruction and applicable safeguards.
- Documented incident containment, assessment, escalation and notifications required by law or contract.
- Human review of material outputs; no confidential client data placed into unapproved public AI tools.
- Supplier and subprocessor due diligence appropriate to the service and data risk.
- Business-continuity practices including calendars, workpapers, handovers and recovery arrangements.
- A simple complaint and privacy-request route through ClearDesk’s published contact channel.

## Commercial and content decisions

- Remove public commercial prices from all service pages.
- Preserve regulatory thresholds, penalties and statutory monetary figures because they are not ClearDesk prices.
- Keep service-package names and inclusions so visitors can self-identify before requesting a quote.
- Explain quote factors in plain language: volume, entities, payroll, overdue periods, system complexity, reporting and support needs.
- Use “expressions of interest” on the Careers page until ClearDesk confirms funded open positions.
- Do not publish invented testimonials, salary comparisons, market-price claims, certifications, government approval or universal legal-compliance claims.

## Limitations and review points

This research supports website content; it is not legal, tax or employment advice. UAE rules may differ for ADGM, DIFC and other sector or free-zone regimes. New Zealand deadlines can vary by filing frequency, balance date, tax-agent status and individual circumstances. Before publishing a specific legal promise or opening a vacancy, ClearDesk should confirm the final operational process and obtain professional advice where appropriate.
