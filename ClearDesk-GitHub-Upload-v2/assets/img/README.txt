THE RULE ON PHOTOGRAPHS OF PEOPLE
---------------------------------
Never put a face on this site that is presented as a ClearDesk person or a ClearDesk
client unless that person really is one and has agreed to it. That applies to
AI-generated faces and to stock-photo faces equally.

- A prospect who reverse-image-searches your "team" and finds a stock photo is gone
  permanently, and in a WhatsApp-forwarding community they will tell people.
- Presenting invented people as your team or clients is a misleading representation
  under the NZ Fair Trading Act 1986 and UAE consumer protection rules.

If you want more photography, use environmental shots that claim nothing:
  Free, licensed for commercial use: unsplash.com and pexels.com
  Good search terms: "Dubai business district", "DIFC", "warehouse logistics",
  "Auckland city", "construction site", "laptop spreadsheet close up",
  "invoice paperwork desk"
  Avoid: any photo where a person's face is the subject, handshake stock photos,
  "businessman pointing at hologram", group meeting shots.
  Never caption a stock photo with a name, a role or a quote.


BEFORE UPLOADING ANY IMAGE
--------------------------
1. Compress it at squoosh.app (free, runs in your browser, no signup).
2. Target sizes: hero 200 KB, section image 120 KB, headshot 100 KB.
3. Give every image meaningful alt text in the HTML — not "image1.jpg".
