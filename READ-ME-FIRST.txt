DO THESE FOUR THINGS BEFORE YOU PUBLISH
---------------------------------------
1. CONFIRM YOUR NZ PRICES
   /nz/pricing/index.html deliberately has NO prices on it, with an honest note
   saying they are being confirmed. Old figures would be misleading under the Fair
   Trading Act. Put your current prices in, or leave the note — both are safe.

2. TEST THE CONTACT OPTIONS
   The contact form opens the visitor's email app with their enquiry prepared for
   info@cleardesk.co.nz. BOTIM buttons copy the ClearDesk number and open the BOTIM app.
   WhatsApp opens a prepared chat. A direct mobile-call link is also included.
   Test all three from your phone after the GitHub preview is live.

   Eranda's profile uses eranda@cleardesk.co.nz and +64 21 087 78576. The main
   ClearDesk contact and BOTIM number remain +64 20 4010 1914.

3. GET THE LEGAL PAGES REVIEWED
   /legal/privacy/ and /legal/terms/ contain publishable website wording, but should
   still be checked against your signed engagement documents and reviewed by an
   appropriately qualified adviser before the main-domain launch.

4. DECIDE ON THE "XERO CERTIFIED" CLAIM
   It appears NOWHERE on this site, on purpose, because it was flagged as unverified.
   Verify it first if you want to use it.


IMPORTANT ACCURACY BOUNDARIES
-----------------------------
Keep future edits accurate, professional and evidence-based:

  - Describe ClearDesk as supporting clients across New Zealand, the United Arab
    Emirates and Sri Lanka through clear scopes and coordinated digital delivery.
  - For UAE e-invoicing, describe ClearDesk's role as readiness, data mapping,
    implementation support and testing alongside the client's accredited provider.
  - Use only qualifications, registrations, people and client feedback that can be
    verified and approved for publication.
  - Avoid guaranteed savings, refund figures or penalty-avoidance promises.
  - Keep general-information notices on tools and technical reference pages.
  - Use only generic, evidence-based comparisons unless a named comparison has been
    reviewed and approved.


HOW TO CHANGE THINGS
--------------------
Text:   open the .html file in any text editor, find the words, change them, save.
Colour: assets/css/styles.css, the :root block at the very top. Change it once and
        it changes everywhere.
Menu:   the header and footer are repeated in every .html file. If you change a menu
        link you have to change it in all 29 files — use Find and Replace across the
        folder, or ask Claude or ChatGPT to do it.

Brand colours in use:
  Navy   #0A1628    Blue  #1B5EAE
  Sky    #4A90D9    Amber #E8A020   (amber is reserved for deadlines and one CTA)

----------
Follow 05-PUBLISHING-GUIDE.md from the pack. Short version:

  2. Drag everything inside this folder into the upload page
  3. Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch > main > / (root)
  4. Test at https://erandaks42194219.github.io/cleardesk-site/
  5. Only then point cleardesk.co.nz at it with the DNS records in the guide
