CLEARDESK WEBSITE — cleardesk.co.nz
====================================
Built 4 September 2026. 30 pages. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
No frameworks, no build step, no npm. You can open any file and edit the text.


HOW TO LOOK AT IT RIGHT NOW
---------------------------
Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser and everything works, including
the readiness check and the mobile menu. Narrow the window right down to see how it
looks on a phone.

DO THESE FIVE THINGS BEFORE YOU PUBLISH
---------------------------------------
1. ADD YOUR PHOTO
   /eranda/index.html has a dashed placeholder box. Save a real headshot as
   assets/img/eranda-headshot.jpg (800x800) and replace that box with:
   <img src="../assets/img/eranda-headshot.jpg" alt="Eranda De Silva, Director of
   ClearDesk Business Services" style="border-radius:12px">

2. CONFIRM YOUR NZ PRICES
   /nz/pricing/index.html deliberately has NO prices on it, with an honest note
   saying they are being confirmed. Old figures would be misleading under the Fair
   Trading Act. Put your current prices in, or leave the note — both are safe.

3. TEST THE CONTACT OPTIONS
   The contact form opens the visitor's email app with their enquiry prepared for
   info@cleardesk.co.nz. BOTIM buttons copy the ClearDesk number and open the BOTIM app.
   Test both from your phone after the GitHub preview is live.

4. GET THE LEGAL PAGES REVIEWED
   /legal/privacy/ and /legal/terms/ are marked DRAFT at the top. You already have a
   Privacy Policy v2.0 and Terms in your GitHub repository — merge them, then have
   the result reviewed. The UAE-facing terms need a UAE-qualified adviser, not the
   NZ version with the country name changed.

5. DECIDE ON THE "XERO CERTIFIED" CLAIM
   It appears NOWHERE on this site, on purpose, because it was flagged as unverified.
   Verify it first if you want to use it.


WHAT THIS SITE DELIBERATELY DOES NOT SAY
----------------------------------------
These are legal boundaries, not style choices. Do not add them back in:

  - It never calls ClearDesk an Accredited Service Provider, ASP, MoF accredited,
    FTA approved or government approved. Every page says the opposite explicitly.
  - It never calls ClearDesk a Tax Agent, in either country. In NZ the site states
    you are a nominated person with Inland Revenue, not a Listed Tax Agent.
  - It never implies a UAE office, address, phone number or local team.
  - It contains no guaranteed savings, refund figures or penalty-avoidance promises.
  - It contains no invented people, clients, testimonials or reviews. There is no
    testimonials section, because you have no consented quotes yet. Add one when you
    have real ones.
  - Every tool and reference page carries a "general information only, not
    personalised advice" notice.
  - No competitor is named or priced. Comparisons use generic market ranges only.


HOW TO CHANGE THINGS
--------------------
Text:   open the .html file in any text editor, find the words, change them, save.
Colour: assets/css/styles.css, the :root block at the very top. Change it once and
        it changes everywhere.
Menu:   the header and footer are repeated in every .html file. If you change a menu
        link you have to change it in all 30 files — use Find and Replace across the
        folder, or ask Claude or ChatGPT to do it.

Brand colours in use:
  Navy   #0A1628    Blue  #1B5EAE
  Sky    #4A90D9    Amber #E8A020   (amber is reserved for deadlines and one CTA)


PUBLISHING
----------
Follow 05-PUBLISHING-GUIDE.md from the pack. Short version:
  1. github.com > new PUBLIC repository called cleardesk-site
  2. Drag everything inside this folder into the upload page
  3. Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch > main > / (root)
  4. Test at https://erandaks4219.github.io/cleardesk-site/
  5. Only then point cleardesk.co.nz at it with the DNS records in the guide
  6. DO NOT touch your MX records — that is your email
