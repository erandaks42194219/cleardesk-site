Fair quotation

What shapes your quote

A smaller, well-organised business should not be priced like a complex catch-up. These are the main workload drivers we review.

Volume

Monthly transactions, bank accounts, invoices, suppliers and employees.

Complexity

Entities, currencies, inventory, integrations, intercompany activity and reporting.

Starting point

How current the records are, data quality and the number of catch-up periods.

Service rhythm

Monthly close timing, AP/AR workflow, payroll and agreed update frequency.

Systems

Your accounting platform, connected apps, migration and automation needs.

Specialist input

Any additional tax, technology or implementation expertise agreed in scope.

Software and third-party provider fees are identified separately. Your proposal will state what ClearDesk includes, what remains with you and what is delivered by another provider.

Start with a short workload review

Tell us what you do, the approximate monthly volume, your systems, employees and whether the records are current. We will prepare a written scope and private quote.
