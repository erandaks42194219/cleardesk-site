Context

What the alternatives cost

Not a competitor comparison — these are the broad market ranges a UAE business owner is choosing between. Prices move, so check current figures before deciding.

8,000–15,000 AED per month for an in-house accountant, before visa costs and end-of-service gratuity — and you carry the recruitment risk
1,500–4,500 AED per month typical for local small-business bookkeeping, with accounting software usually billed separately at AED 110–280 a month
799–2,999 AED per month, ClearDesk, all in — the difference is the Auckland cost base and the eight-hour time difference, not a discount

Software is not included in our fees. Xero, MYOB and similar subscriptions are separate. You either hold your own, or we arrange it for you at cost with no margin added. We would rather be clear about that up front than build it into a headline price.
