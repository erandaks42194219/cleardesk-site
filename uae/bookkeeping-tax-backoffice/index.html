If you are behind

Catch-up work, done once, properly

Twelve to thirty-six months of unreconciled records is a normal position for a business that has been busy. It is fixable, and it is better fixed before 1 January 2027 than after.

We rebuild the records rather than patching them: bank reconciled month by month, supplier and customer ledgers reconstructed, VAT positions recalculated, and the whole thing handed over as a branded computation with a colour-coded workbook behind it so anyone can follow the workings later.

Most clients move onto a monthly desk at handover, because the reason the records fell behind in the first place has not gone away.

The boundary

What we do not do

Worth reading before you engage anyone, not after.

Not within our scope

  • Audit or assurance
  • Acting as a UAE Tax Agent registered with the FTA
  • Transfer pricing studies
  • Free zone qualifying income opinions
  • Economic substance regulation filings
  • Personalised tax or legal advice

What that means in practice

We prepare. Where a filing legally requires a registered agent, that filing is made by you or by a registered agent, and we will tell you clearly when you have reached that line rather than quietly stepping over it.

Where a job is outside our scope we will say so and, if we can, point you to someone who does it properly. We would rather lose the work than do it badly.
